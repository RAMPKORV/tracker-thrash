TRACKER TRASH: FIELD MANUAL

WHAT IS THIS

A demoscene toy that plays tracker modules with libopenmpt and draws what the notes are doing: chords, key, piano roll, pattern grid, channel VU, and a look-ahead forecast. Plus an unreasonable cartoon butt.

DROP A FILE

Anything libopenmpt supports: .mod .xm .s3m .it .mptm .mtm .669 .far .med .okt .stm .ult .mo3 .umx and friends. Nothing is uploaded. Everything runs in your browser.

KEYS

Space play/pause · ←/→ seek 5s · R restart · O open · Z Monsquaz mode · M full music panels · S serious · H HUD · G gross toggle · F fullscreen · V next background · ? this

READING THE HUD

By default the music lives in the scene: the chord is a monument/sign/rune, notes are physical objects, channel activity is pipes/KPI bars/CRT columns, and the next chord is foreshadowed in-world. Press M for the full piano roll, pattern grid and pitch-class clock when you want the x-ray view.

SERIOUS MODE

Keeps the demoscene and the theory, removes almost all gags and the poop engine. For when a musician walks in, or you are at work.

MONSQUAZ MODE

A music-synced recreation of the classic four-phase Monsquaz loop: up, sunset dance, down, zoom. The original choreography is by the Monsquaz project; this version uses the actual non-explicit up/down backgrounds, arrows, sunset, side GIF, and head crop, plus original art, with the module as its soundtrack. The head spins in 3D during the zoom. The original asset is NSFW and is not bundled. Press Z or use the MONSQ button.

ACCESSIBILITY

Flash-safe by default: no rapid full-screen flashes, capped shake, reduced motion respected. The chaos slider goes to 0. If you are photosensitive, keep it low. The captions and HUD are text, not baked into the canvas.
